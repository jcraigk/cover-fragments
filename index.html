Cover fragments inside a host song: song, jam, or tease?

When a cover fragment shows up inside another song, the setlist uses one of three labels:

  1. Song A full song entry (sometimes marked Incomplete): Runaway Jim -> Gypsy Queen -> Runaway Jim
  2. Jam A "{Song} Jam" entry: Possum -> I Can't Turn You Loose Jam > Possum
  3. Tease A tease footnote: "Hood included a Moby Dick tease"

Similar musical events get different labels on different performances, and the label drives cover play counts, gap and bustout stats, debut credits, and song pages.
